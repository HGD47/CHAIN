History

Every pull adds to a running history instead of replacing it. This controls how many days back to keep before an article is quietly dropped from local storage.

Automatic pulls

Off by default — Wire only ever fetches when you click "Pull latest." Turning this on will also pull automatically while the app stays open, which helps catch stories that would otherwise scroll off a fast-moving outlet's feed between your visits. It can't retrieve anything from while your computer was off or the app was closed.
